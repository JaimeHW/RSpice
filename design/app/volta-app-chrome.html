Console — four tabs, two kinds of row

Console · Errors · Warnings filter the same log; Automation is a different animal — a command line pinned to the bottom with its history above. Severity counts ride the tab labels. The body is in the hero — switch its tabs, collapse it, type into Automation (help works).
