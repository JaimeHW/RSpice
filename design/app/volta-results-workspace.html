Design notes — Results workspace

The strip grammar

Every plot in the product is a strip: a 28 px header (kind tag · subtitle · legend chips · actions) over a document-well body. Stacked strips share X alignment and are separated by 1 px of --border. The same grammar carries every analysis viewer, so users learn it once.

Numbers never float on traces

Values live in three sanctioned places: the hover readout (transient), cursor blocks and tables in the right panel (persistent), and small bordered marker tags pinned to analysis features (UGF, HDn, ±σ). The canvas stays legible at a glance.

Cursors

A is --accent, B is --trace-5 — solid 1 px verticals with a 12 px letter flag at the top edge. Per-trace intercepts and B−A deltas render in the right panel, including 1/Δx for frequency-from-time. Hover crosshair stays dashed --text-faint so it never competes with placed cursors.

Right panel is the instrument readout

Each viewer swaps the right panel to its own stats: cursors+measurements (waves), margins (bode), THD/harmonics (fft), eye metrics+mask verdict (eye), distribution+yield (hist). Tables use the shared mtable; key results are accent-highlighted rows, one or two per table at most.

Open items

Strip drag-to-reorder and Y-axis drag-zoom are interaction details deferred to implementation. Decimation strategy (per-pixel min/max envelope) matters for 1 M+ point transients and is noted in the mapping table.
