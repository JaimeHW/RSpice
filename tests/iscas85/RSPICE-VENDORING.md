# RSpice ISCAS85 Vendoring Notes

This directory vendors the ISCAS85 combinational benchmark circuits as
transistor-level SPICE, used by
`crates/rspice-conformance/src/suites/execution.rs`.

## ⚠ Licensing is unresolved — read before shipping

**The upstream archive contains no license file and no license header on any
retained file.** This is not "permissively licensed"; it is *unlicensed*,
which for a commercial product is the more restrictive position of the two.
The material has three separate provenances:

- **The netlists** (`85/`) are machine-generated: their headers read
  `generated by spicegen.pl 1.0 / by Jingye Xu @ VLSI group, Dept of ECE,
  UIC`. The underlying ISCAS85 benchmark set has been redistributed openly
  since 1985, but the generator and these SPICE renderings carry no stated
  terms.
- **`technology/45nm_HP.pm`** is a Predictive Technology Model card from
  Arizona State University (<http://ptm.asu.edu>). PTM is published for free
  download; the site states no redistribution grant.
- **`libraries/stdcells.lib`** identifies itself as `wsclib 0.13um values`
  with no attribution beyond that.

RSpice's position: this corpus is retained for internal validation, and a
decision on redistribution is **owed before any release that ships the
`tests/` tree**. The options if the status cannot be cleared are to fetch it
on demand in CI rather than vendoring it, or to substitute an equivalently
sized corpus with known terms. Do not treat the absence of a complaint as a
grant.

## Source

- Downloaded from <https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/tests/iscas85Circuits.7z>
  on 2026-07-28, linked from ngspice's "Tests and quality assurance" page at
  <https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/quality.html>.
- The upstream directory shape is preserved because the decks depend on it:
  each netlist reaches `../../technology/45nm_HP.pm` and
  `../../libraries/*.lib` by relative include.

## Why this corpus

Scale, which nothing else vendored here provides. The largest deck in
`tests/ngspice/` is a few hundred lines; `85/c7552/c7552_ann.net` is ~89,000
lines and instantiates thousands of MOSFETs over a 45nm BSIM4 card, with
extracted parasitic capacitance on every net. These are the decks where
matrix ordering, device-evaluation throughput, and timestep control stop
being theoretical, and they are also transistor-level digital — a regime the
analog regression corpora barely touch.

Circuits, by gate count: c432 (160), c499 (202), c880 (383), c1355 (546),
c1908 (880), c2670 (1193), c3540 (1669), c5315 (2307), c6288 (2416, a 16×16
multiplier and the traditional hard case), c7552 (3512). Each has a plain
variant and an `_ann` variant back-annotated with parasitics.

## Trimmed upstream material

- `iscas85.pdf` — the published ISCAS'85 benchmark paper. Third-party
  copyrighted text with no bearing on simulation; not redistributed.
- Editor backup files (`*.net~`, `*.lib~`).
- `85/c1355p/`, `85/c5315p/`, `85/c6288p/` — byte-identical duplicates of
  their unsuffixed directories. `85/c7552p/` is *not* a duplicate (it differs
  in `.OPTIONS` and adds `.param` drive levels) and is retained.

## Harness status

Run by `crates/rspice-conformance/tests/execution_corpora.rs` against
`execution-manifest.tsv` in this directory. There is no reference data —
upstream's validation is a `plot` command in a `.control` block — so the
contract is execution, not conformance.

Two properties of these decks are worth knowing before editing the manifest:

- The annotated variants request their transient **only from inside a
  `.control` block**, guarded by `if $?batchmode`. There is no bare `.tran`
  card. The suite therefore parses with control blocks intact and relies on
  the parser promoting the analysis out; stripping them first makes these
  decks appear to request nothing.
- Cost spans three orders of magnitude across the corpus, so the expensive
  decks are marked `!extended` in the manifest and skipped unless
  `RSPICE_EXECUTION_EXTENDED=1` is set.
