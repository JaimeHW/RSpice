Release notes vA-code of L_UTSOI 102.9.0 (January 2026)
----------------------------------------------------

Changes from L-UTSOI102.8 to L-UTSOI102.9:

Bug fixes
- BF1: NQS calculation: The second pole implementation has been updated to reduce large stamping values.
- BF2: Update the descriptions of the parameters and the unused macro.

Accuracy and predictability improvements
- AP1: Introduction of gate resistance (introduction of RG for local model and RGO, RINT, RVPOLY, RSHG for global mode).
- AP2: Introduction of source, drain and well resistance (introduction of RSE, RDE and RWELL for local model and RSH, RSHD and RWELLO for global mode).

Changes in model inputs and outputs
- IO1: MOS gate, source, drain and well resistance OP-variables has been updated in accordance with introduction of parasitic resistances.
- IO2: Introduction of MULT_I, MULT_Q and MULT_FN.
- IO3: Introduction of NGCON, XGW, NRS and NRD.
